Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Panelhouse Studios ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit panelhousestudios.com (the "Site") or place an order, submit a commission request, or sign up for our newsletter.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, and shipping address when you place an order or request a commission
  • Email address when you sign up for our newsletter
  • Payment information at checkout, handled by our payment processor — we do not store full card numbers on our own servers
  • Any reference art, descriptions, or messages you submit through the commission form

Cookies & Local Storage

The Site uses your browser's local storage to remember your cart contents and any print reviews you submit. This data stays in your own browser and is not automatically synced to a server or shared with other visitors. We do not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to process and ship orders, respond to commission requests, send order and shipping confirmations, send newsletter updates (only if you signed up), and improve the Site.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us run the business — for example, a payment processor to handle checkout and a shipping carrier to deliver your order — and only to the extent needed for them to perform that service.

Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email we send. To make a request, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to commissions@panelhousestudios.com.
