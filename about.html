How Every Edition Is Made

01
Source

We license the panel directly from the mangaka or publisher — no scans, no fan reprints.

02
Restore

Original art is cleaned and color-matched at full resolution before it ever touches a press.

03
Print

Giclée printed on 100% cotton rag stock, the same process fine-art galleries use.

04
Number

Each print is hand-numbered against its edition size and never reprinted once it sells out.

Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Standard shipping is $6 flat, free on orders over $150, and arrives in 7–18 days. Express shipping is +$18 and arrives in 2–3 days. Every print ships rolled in a rigid tube to keep it flat and undamaged.

Returns

Because every edition is hand-numbered and printed to order, we can't accept returns for a change of mind. If a print arrives damaged or misprinted, contact us within 14 days of delivery for a free reprint or full refund.
