About / JSMANGA

We read it before we print it.

JSMANGA started as a shelf of posters nobody could agree on hanging up — half of us wanted key art, half of us wanted the panel nobody talks about. So we do both: every print in the shop is picked because a chapter earned it, and every review exists so you're not buying a poster for a story you'd actually hate.

Prints are reproduced at archival quality on 200gsm matte stock, printed to order in small batches — nothing sits in a warehouse losing color for a year before it reaches you.
